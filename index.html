IPA Translator

International Phonetic Alphabet Translator

Translate text to International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) notation. Supports 24 languages with character-by-character and phrase-based translation.

About

This open-source project translates text from various languages to International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) notation. It uses IPA databases and romanization conversion tables to provide accurate phonetic transcriptions.
